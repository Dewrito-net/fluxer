Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

Summary

We take your privacy seriously. Here are the key points:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data
  • We do not train AI models on your messages, files, or content
  • The service is not end-to-end encrypted, but we use strong encryption for data in transit (TLS)
  • We collect only what's necessary to operate the service
  • You can export, manage, and delete your data through your account settings

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address, username, password (stored securely using Argon2id hashing), and date of birth
  • Content: Messages, files, images, voice communications, community information, reactions, and profile data
  • Payment information: Processed securely by Stripe. We do not store full payment card details

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type
  • Usage information: Features used, session duration, crash reports and performance metrics
  • Security logs: Login attempts, authentication events, rate limits, and abuse detection signals
  • Push notification tokens: Device tokens for delivering mobile push notifications (e.g., Firebase Cloud Messaging)

We use this information to secure the service, detect abuse, and improve performance. We do not use it for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Dewrito platform
  • To create and manage your account
  • To deliver your messages and content to intended recipients
  • To secure your account and prevent unauthorized access
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and spam
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your messages or content for targeted advertising or AI training.

3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following cases:

  • When you direct us to: Sending messages, joining communities, sharing content with other users
  • Service providers: Infrastructure hosting, payment processing (Stripe), content delivery (Cloudflare), CSAM scanning (Arachnid Shield), error monitoring (Sentry), GIF search (Klipy, routed through our servers), and email services
  • Legal requirements: When required by valid legal process or to protect safety

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers located in the United States. We use encryption for data in transit (TLS). We implement access controls, rate limiting, and regular security updates.

5. Content Scanning

We use the Arachnid Shield API to scan user-uploaded media against databases of known child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This is hash-based matching only, not AI content analysis. Detected content is immediately rejected and reported to authorities as required by law.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is kept while your account is active
  • Deleted content: Removed from active systems promptly; may persist in backups for up to 90 days
  • Inactive accounts: May be deleted after 2 years of inactivity with advance notice
  • Payment data: Retained as required by accounting and tax obligations

7. Your Privacy Controls

Through your account settings and privacy dashboard, you can:

  • Request a data export of your account information
  • Delete individual messages or bulk-delete all messages
  • Delete your account entirely

You can also email admin@dewrito.net for specific data requests.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data

9. Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. Any cookies we set are strictly necessary for the operation and security of the service. Third-party embeds (e.g., YouTube videos) may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Users must meet the minimum age requirement (typically 13) in their region. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the minimum age. If we discover a child's account, we will delete it and associated data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. For significant changes, we'll provide at least 30 days' notice. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at admin@dewrito.net.
